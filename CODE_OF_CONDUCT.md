# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews is expected to:

- **Be respectful and constructive.** Critique the code or the idea, not the person.
- **Argue from evidence.** Settle disagreements with sources, tests and reproductions, not volume or status.
- **Assume good faith.** Most mistakes are honest. Correct them kindly.
- **Stay on topic and professional.** No harassment, personal attacks, discrimination or spam.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment of any kind, publishing other people's private information, sustained disruption, and deliberately submitting false or misleading content.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions) and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately to the maintainers by email at **admin@tweakeo.com**, through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**), which opens a private thread, or with GitHub's [report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainers (see [GOVERNANCE.md](.github/GOVERNANCE.md)) may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening or harmful.
