Trimmed for a project with one maintainer: the Maintainer Council, voting, meetings and CNCF resources are removed, and decisions follow the declared-leader model that [Open Source Guides](https://opensource.guide/leadership-and-governance/) describes.

This governance explains how the project is run.

## Values

* Openness: Communication and decision-making happens in the open and is discoverable for future
  reference. As much as possible, all discussions and work take place in public
  forums and open repositories.

* Fairness: All stakeholders have the opportunity to provide feedback and submit
  contributions, which will be considered on their merits.

* Inclusivity: We innovate through different perspectives and skill sets, which
  can only be accomplished in a welcoming and respectful environment.

## Maintainers

Maintainers have write access to the project repository. The current maintainers
can be found in [MAINTAINERS.md](./MAINTAINERS.md).

The project has one maintainer, who decides on scope, reviews and merges, and releases.
AI agents may draft changes and pull requests; a maintainer reviews every one, and only a
maintainer merges.

### Becoming a Maintainer

A new maintainer is invited by the current maintainer, after a record of reviewed
contributions to the project. The new maintainer is added to MAINTAINERS.md in a pull request.

### Removing a Maintainer

Maintainers may resign at any time if they feel that they will not be able to
continue fulfilling their project duties.

### Emeritus Maintainers

Depending on the reason for removal or resignation, a Maintainer may be converted
to Emeritus status. Emeritus Maintainers are recognized for their past contributions
and may still be consulted on project matters, but do not have merge access.
Emeritus Maintainers are listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](./MAINTAINERS.md)
under a separate Emeritus section.

## Code of Conduct

[Code of Conduct](https://github.com/pacocartones/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) violations are handled by the maintainer, through
the contact the Code of Conduct gives.

## Security Response Team

The maintainer handles all reports of security holes and breaches according to the
[security policy](SECURITY.md).

## When to Evolve This Governance

When a second maintainer joins, this file is replaced by the CNCF maintainer-council
template, trimmed, with a rule for breaking ties between two maintainers.

## Modifying this Charter

Changes to this Governance are made by the maintainer in a pull request.
